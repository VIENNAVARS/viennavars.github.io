Protecting your personal data is a priority of the VIENNAVARS research project. We process technical connection data strictly in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / DSGVO, Regulation [EU] 2016/679), the Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG), and the Austrian Telecommunications Act (TKG 2021).

4. Contact via Email & Scientific Data Access Requests (PHAIDRA o:2091644) If you contact the project team via email or submit a request for scientific access to controlled research datasets archived in PHAIDRA (o:2091644), the personal data you provide (name, institutional affiliation, email address, and research inquiry) will be processed exclusively for the purpose of handling and verifying your academic request (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Note on Bioarchaeological & Historical Data (Recital 27 GDPR) All anthropological, osteological, and archaeological data presented on this website refer exclusively to deceased individuals from the early medieval Avar period (7th–8th centuries AD). Pursuant to Recital 27 GDPR, the General Data Protection Regulation does not apply to the data of deceased persons; ethical handling and spatial aggregation follow professional archaeological and bioanthropological standards.
